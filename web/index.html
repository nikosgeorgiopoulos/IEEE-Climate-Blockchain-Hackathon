How it works
1
SignEvery reading is serialized canonically and signed with Ed25519 — on the device, or at the gateway the moment it is ingested.
2
BatchEvery few minutes, all new readings are grouped into a batch.
3
Merkle rootEach signed reading becomes a leaf; one 32-byte root commits to all of them.
4
AnchorThe root goes on a public blockchain in one transaction. Anyone can then verify any reading — and any later edit shows up.
